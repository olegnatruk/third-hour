# Third Hour website imagery

The eleven JPGs in this folder were downloaded on 2026-09-01 from the public **THIRD HOUR CAFE** Facebook page supplied by the project owner:

- https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61577136480938

They are kept as source candidates for Third Hour's own public website. The source page labels them as coffee, drinks, desserts, and sandwiches. They are shown in the landing-page gallery and should not be repurposed outside Third Hour without the owners' approval.

`paper-walnut-texture.png` is a Codex-generated material texture, created for this project with this prompt:

> seamless near-black handmade paper and smoked walnut texture, tiny irregular fibers, faint film grain, almost imperceptible warm bronze highlights, completely abstract, no objects, no text, no logo; premium editorial material swatch; black coffee, deep espresso, muted taupe, understated antique gold.
