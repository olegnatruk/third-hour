# Product Overview

## Third Hour

Third Hour is a café project with two connected digital products:

1. **Café website** — a public home for the café, its identity, location, menu, and customer information.
2. **Loyalty card** — a customer experience for earning and using café rewards.

## Product goals

- Make it easy for customers to discover and visit Third Hour.
- Give regular customers a simple reason to return.
- Create a warm, consistent café identity across both experiences.

## Loyalty-card first release

- Customers create an account and sign in with a mobile number and password.
- Customers can see their own stamp balance and a virtual loyalty-card QR code.
- Admins can view registered customers, scan a customer QR code, and award stamps.
- SMS verification and one-time passwords are not part of the initial release.

## Still to define

- Café location, operating hours, and launch timing.
- Brand voice, colors, logo, photography, and menu content.
- Loyalty mechanics: how customers earn, redeem, and identify themselves.
- Loyalty rules: stamps required for a reward, reward types, and redemption process.
- Admin and cashier account onboarding.
- QR token lifetime and whether an online connection is required for scanning.
